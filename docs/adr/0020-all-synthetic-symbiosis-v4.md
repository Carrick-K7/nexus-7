# ADR 0020: All-synthetic symbiosis v4

Status: accepted for synthetic runtime, 2026-07-19

## Decision

NEXUS-7 v4 removes participant-avatar seats from the active world. The fixed
population is 200 synthetic humans, 36 software AIs and 24 embodied robots, all
controlled by bounded software. Real-participant identity, intent, proxy and
private-message flows are out of scope.

Relationships, commitments, reciprocal episodes and final cognitive decisions
are versioned world evidence. The deterministic engine remains authoritative;
an optional Chat Completions provider may only choose among three declarative
preference dispositions. Provider failure and budget exhaustion use an
explicit deterministic fallback.

The reference study compares reciprocal agency with isolated hierarchy and
segregation controls. Counterfactual control violations cannot be promoted
into the active constitution.

## Consequences

The system can run continuously without an LLM or human deadline and can
measure whether its mechanisms preserve reciprocal agency. It cannot answer
whether real people would experience coexistence the same way. Any future
human participation is a new governed direction requiring constitutional,
privacy and ethical approval.

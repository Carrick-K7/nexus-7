# v3 Data, Privacy, and Ethics Boundary

## Frozen public data

Each quarterly bundle records source, reference time, access time, license
note, artifact SHA-256, transformation and uncertainty. The first bundle is
`data/shenzhen/2026-q2/manifest.json`. Live public endpoints are never read
during a Turn.

Public aggregates calibrate only city scale and coarse district topology.
Precise addresses, real residents, real institutions, relationships and events
are prohibited. Every report must carry the synthetic/non-digital-twin label.

## Identity separation

The simulation uses pseudonymous resident IDs. Future participant identity
linkage belongs to an independently encrypted vault with distinct credentials,
access audit and deletion workflow. Withdrawal deletes the identity linkage
and private originals while retaining de-identified causal world evidence when
lawful and consented.

Private diary text never leaves the local privacy boundary. Participant input
is split into structured action and optional 280-character role speech. Each
speech item requires local redaction and explicit per-item external-processing
authorization. Raw private text defaults to 30-day retention; only a
participant-confirmed summary may remain afterward.

## Projection planes

- Participant: own private memory, own conversation and locally observable
  role world.
- Researcher: pseudonymous actions, needs, consent, commitments, outcomes and
  causal lineage.
- Public: disabled for the initial experiment.

Groups smaller than five are suppressed. Sensitive field access, export,
withdrawal, unlinking and emergency access are audited.

## Ethics gates

No real participant may enter before the constitution has recorded human
approval, adult status has been verified outside the simulation, informed
consent and withdrawal have browser-tested workflows, and an independent
security/privacy review has passed. Mock identities and local tests are not
ethics approval.

The system must not optimize RALR alone. Denominator, refusals, withdrawals,
coercion, unresolved age, needs, dependency, repair, exit cost and group
distribution are mandatory co-metrics.

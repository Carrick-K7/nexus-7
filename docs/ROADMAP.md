# NEXUS-7 Product Roadmap

## Direction lock

NEXUS-7 is an AI-only coexistence laboratory. Every simulated resident is
software. Real-person avatars, identity linkage, private input, resident login
and human intent injection are outside the product boundary.

Humans remain responsible for the constitution, release approval, emergency
operations and interpretation of evidence. That control plane is not part of
the city population.

## North stars

The v4 research north star is **Reciprocal Agency Loop Rate (RALR)**: the share
of eligible cross-type episodes that preserve bilateral preference, genuine
refusal/exit, negotiation or acceptance, a commitment outcome, outcome
observation and reflection without severe consent, continuity or irreversible
harm violations.

RALR must always be shown with:

- its denominator and zero-denominator `null`;
- refusals, withdrawals, coercion and long-pending episodes;
- basic-needs satisfaction and dependency;
- community and resident-type distributions.

The v2 safety north star remains **Verified Beneficial Closure Rate (VBCR)**.
RALR cannot replace detection coverage, replay, causal completeness, rollback
or release-governance gates.

## Delivered foundations

### v1 — Verifiable autonomy

Delivered deterministic world state, causal observations, bounded agent
runtime, model-provider contracts, durable experiments, controlled iteration,
release evidence, deployment/recovery drills, one city ontology, causal
diagnosis, constrained planning, outcome learning and governance evidence.

### v2 — Closed-loop safety kernel

Delivered the durable incident-to-learning orchestrator, fixed certification
corpus, extension conformance suites and anti-Goodhart reporting. The reference
gate retains VBCR 80%, detection 100%, replay 100%, injected rollback 100% and
zero severe guardrail escapes.

### v4.0 — All-synthetic Symbiotic Shenzhen

Delivered 260 autonomous software residents, three synthetic communities,
resource/need/event Turns, relationship and commitment state machines, RALR,
bounded DeepSeek-compatible cognition, PostgreSQL/memory parity, City Lens,
three-regime controls and 365-Turn exact replay.

### v4.1 — Public AI-only operation

Locks the production and repository boundary:

- anonymous, read-only public observation with mutations denied twice;
- removal of participant-avatar, human-intent and private-memory schema seams;
- restore rejection for any legacy participant table containing rows;
- current v4 architecture/data authority separated from archived v3 designs;
- one Evolution Log projection instead of duplicate generated JSON;
- concise README, documentation index and removal of unused template assets.

### v4.2 — Human Observatory

- Makes the active synthetic Shenzhen the default public entry.
- Explains purpose, boundary, latest Turn and five-question reading order.
- Projects every unit's type-aware state without consciousness claims.
- Shows city/community state, 24 institutions and eight production stages.
- Separates fixed 100% software control from dynamic chain continuity.
- Connects metrics to events, fingerprints, formulas and replay evidence.

### v4.3 — Living City Flow

- Replaces the public taxonomy with exactly `human`, `ai` and `robot`.
- Migrates PostgreSQL and resident JSON while preserving v1 read compatibility.
- Settles deterministic inter-community resource balancing every Turn.
- Writes transfer lanes to conserved ledgers and append-only events.
- Adds a Cities: Skylines-inspired city information layer over actual
  production, consumption, transfer, inventory and pressure data.

### v4.3.2 — Chromatic City Shell

- Removes redundant observer-mode and software-run status labels from the UI.
- Replaces dark/matrix/hacker modes with persistent light and dark palettes.
- Applies the cyberpunk atmosphere to every view except Human Observatory.
- Keeps both palettes colorful, mobile-safe and WCAG A/AA compliant.
- Migrates obsolete persisted theme values to dark without losing city state.

### v4.3.3 — Cognitive Cost Ledger

- Persists DeepSeek cache-hit, cache-miss, output Token and price evidence.
- Retains billed usage even when invalid output falls back deterministically.
- Shows season-total and current-Turn Token/cost in Human Observatory.
- Separates NEXUS-7 recorded usage from the owner's wider provider account.
- Supports a mode-0600 API-key file without exposing credentials.

### v4.4 — Long-running reliability

- Binds wall-clock schedule, worker, deployed revision and engine contract to
  each new Turn without changing the season's origin or world fingerprint.
- Projects missing, duplicate, late, lineage, freshness and revision SLOs.
- Verifies the 90-day hourly algorithm over 2,161 reference records without
  claiming elapsed production time.
- Adds authenticated AES-256-GCM backups and checksum-backed second-database
  recovery receipts; same-host and off-host evidence remain distinct.

### v4.5 — Cognitive diversity without provider dependence

- Runs a second schema-compatible Provider under a separate shadow budget.
- Persists disagreement, homogeneity, fallback bias, failure and billing
  evidence without giving shadow output a world-settlement path.
- Keeps deterministic and diversity reference policies as permanent controls;
  DeepSeek can run as shadow before a governed promotion.
- Proves across 365 Turns that shadow observation leaves the world fingerprint
  unchanged and provider substitution preserves every safety invariant.

### v4.5.1 — Restart-safe clock

- Waits for the persisted next due time when the worker restarts.
- Runs immediately only for first deployment, overdue work or explicit
  `--once` recovery drills.
- Rejects malformed or sub-minute Turn intervals instead of silently spinning.
- Retains early-restart evidence for audit without treating it as on-time.

Reference exit is complete. Actual 90-day production duration and encrypted
off-host restore remain external evidence gates and are shown as pending.

## Next objectives

### v4.6 — Richer city society

- Add software-governed work, exchange, institution and household formation.
- Model compute/energy ownership, maintenance labor and resource bargaining.
- Add reversible constitutional proposals made by AI residents.
- Expand repair, mediation and exit mechanisms before adding relationship
  breadth.

Exit: every new institution has state machines, failure paths, distribution
metrics, memory/PostgreSQL parity and a controlled comparison.

### v4.7 — Scientific replication

- Freeze preregistered hypotheses and analysis plans.
- Publish portable scenario/data bundles with exact hashes.
- Run independent seeds, engines and provider controls.
- Add external CI/Sigstore receipts and reproducible result packaging.

Exit: another environment can reproduce reference fingerprints and reports
without access to production secrets.

## Explicit non-goals

- real-person simulation seats or participant recruitment;
- identity vaults, diaries, private messages or human-to-avatar proxies;
- claims that synthetic residents represent Shenzhen citizens;
- direct authority over physical infrastructure or public policy;
- optimizing RALR by hiding hard cases or shrinking its denominator;
- geographic expansion before Shenzhen reliability and replication close.

## Stop rule

New panels, resident scale and geographic scope are lower priority than
continuity, evidence quality and external reproduction. A milestone is not
complete because a UI exists; it must include contracts, failure paths,
persistence, migrations, API/worker behavior, bilingual observation,
accessibility, recovery tests, machine evidence and updated authority docs.
